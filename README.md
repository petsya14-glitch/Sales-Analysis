# Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, and customer trends across **Furniture** and **Electronics** categories.

## Key Dashboard Views
* **Sales Overview:** High-level KPIs tracking total revenue, order volume, and overall performance.
* **Profit Analysis:** Margin breakdowns and profitability comparisons across categories.
* **Product Analysis:** Top-performing and underperforming items within Furniture and Electronics.
* **Customer Analysis:** Purchasing patterns, customer segmentation, and repeat-buyer trends.
* **Regional Analysis:** Geographic distribution of sales and profit across regions.
* **Time Analysis:** Month-over-month and year-over-year revenue growth and seasonal trends.

## Tools Used
* **Power BI Desktop** (Data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive visualizations)
